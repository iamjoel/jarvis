# Company Success Deep Dive

## Scope defaults

A company name is enough to begin. Unless specified, cover its principal markets, the latest three to five years plus relevant founding decisions, and write for a general business reader in the user's language. Adjust depth to the request. Ask only if the company is ambiguous or a missing choice would materially change the investigation; state reasonable scope assumptions and continue otherwise.

## Investigation

- Define success using relevant evidence: growth, profitability, retention, market position or another outcome. Test whether the premise holds in the selected period.
- Trace the mechanisms connecting customer demand, company choices, distribution, operations and economics to that outcome.
- Choose analytical frameworks for their explanatory value. Industry structure may benefit from Five Forces; durable advantage from 7 Powers; profit capture from value-chain or unit-economic analysis. Include the dimensions that explain this company.
- Prioritize filings, operating metrics, product documentation and direct testimony. Check central or disputed claims against independent evidence when available; explain the limitations of a single source.
- Examine counterevidence, failure conditions and what competitors could reproduce.

[Research plan](templates/research-plan.md) offers prompts for extensive investigations. Keep the plan in working context unless persistence or handoff would help. [Report template](templates/report.md) provides an adaptable output structure; short requests can use a summary, the strongest mechanisms and risks.

## Evidence and delivery

Cite material facts near the claim and keep a sources log. Explicitly label consequential inference, disputed interpretations and evidence gaps. Ordinary sourced facts need no repeated evidence-type label. Use estimates only when there is a defensible method and sourced inputs; state the method and uncertainty. Leave unavailable metrics unknown.

Save the completed report to `data/company-reports/<Company Name>.md` relative to the repository root, with a safe filename. Follow the project's high-density summary and index rules, reviewing `data/readme.md`. A research request authorizes this save; follow an explicit request for draft review before saving when present.
