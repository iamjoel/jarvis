# Platform Considerations

Use these prompts to adapt requested content. They are format choices, not claims about current ranking algorithms. Verify changing product limits or features against current platform documentation when those details matter.

| Platform | Formats to consider | Adaptation decisions |
|----------|---------------------|----------------------|
| LinkedIn | Professional observations, announcements, document posts | Establish the work context and make the first lines understandable before expansion. Use supplied evidence and experience. |
| Twitter/X | Short observations, announcements, threads | Keep each post understandable, and use a thread only when the material needs several connected posts. |
| Instagram | Images, carousels, short videos, Stories | Coordinate the visual and caption; identify what must be communicated on the image or in the video. |
| TikTok | Short videos, demonstrations, narrated examples | Plan an opening that establishes the subject, readable on-screen text, and footage the creator can realistically produce. |
| Facebook | Community updates, local announcements, group discussions, video | Include practical context such as audience, location, date, and action where relevant. |

Choose posting frequency and timing using audience data, goals, and production capacity. Propose a manageable experiment when data is missing. Avoid fixed promises about reach, preferred formats, link placement, or engagement thresholds.

Put links where readers can use them and the platform supports them. Credit creators and tag people when relevant and authorized. A draft can suggest an interaction; executing a comment, tag notification, or message follows the user's actual authorization.
